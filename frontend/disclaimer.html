Important context

How to understand MATLAS results

MATLAS results are generated from self-reported answers and are intended for education and personal reflection.

Uncertainty

Every result contains uncertainty. A score can be affected by mood, memory, interpretation of a question, cultural context, and the situation in which the answers were given. A result is not a permanent label and should not be treated as a measurement of a person’s complete identity.

Scope

DASS-21 is a screening measure. Big Five uses public-domain IPIP items. MBTI-style, Enneagram, and Social Energy are MATLAS reflection tools, not official licensed instruments.

Safety

MATLAS does not diagnose mental-health conditions or replace professional care. If you are worried about your wellbeing, speak with a qualified professional. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis service in your area.
