DASS-21

DASS-21 is a self-report scale covering depression, anxiety, and stress. It asks how much each statement applied during the past week and organizes responses into three symptom dimensions.
Read more about DASS-21 on Wikipedia

IPIP-50 Big Five

The Big Five, also called the five-factor model or OCEAN, describes personality across openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism. These are continuous dimensions, so people usually show a blend of trait levels.
Read more about the Big Five on Wikipedia

MBTI-style preferences

This MBTI-style assessment explores four preference pairs: introversion or extraversion, sensing or intuition, thinking or feeling, and judging or perceiving. The combinations produce familiar four-letter patterns.
Read more about MBTI on Wikipedia

Enneagram

The Enneagram of Personality is commonly presented as nine interconnected personality styles, each associated with characteristic motivations, fears, and behavior patterns.
Read more about the Enneagram on Wikipedia

Introvert, extrovert, or ambivert?

Introversion and extraversion describe a broad personality dimension related to social engagement and stimulation. Many people fall somewhere between the two, so this reflection reports a tendency toward introvert, extrovert, or ambivert energy rather than a fixed identity.
Read more about introversion and extraversion on Wikipedia
